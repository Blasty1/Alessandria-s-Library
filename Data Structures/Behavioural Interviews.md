The company wants to know if the candidate can  work effectively and successfully in the company's environment.
They are evaluating the company culture fit.
Key signals:
1. CV Check: they will check my CV and they will ask detailed information about something.
2. Leadership: taking initiative, being proactive and leading others.
3. Collaboration: the ability to collaborate effectively.
	1. Being open and transparent to your team
	2. Empathy is key
	3. Collaboration style may change from company to company 
	4. Conflict resolution is also important, we should expecte at least one question about that.
4. Communication: explain well our ideas.
	1. Break down complex ideas
	2. Ask good questions
	3. Share progress and give feedbacks
5. Passion/Motivation: check if we do actually care about the work or it just chasing a paycheck.
	1. Open to learn from failures or from the others
	2. Seeking opportunities to grow 
	3. Showing curiosities and not being loud 
6. Resilience: how we respond when things don't go to our way
	1. They want to know if we can stay calm under pressure 
	2. A good signal is someone who does not blame others when things go wrong but instead reflects, regroups and comes back with a better plan.

Preparation plan for behavioural interview:
1. Create a google doc and brainstorm.
	1. Our goal is to surface ay relevant experience we had ( list all the projects/jobs that can highlight qualities that are assessed on this interview )
	2. We think of those experiences as short stories that highlight our qualities , we write them as a short bullet lists ( not word for word ).
2. Categorize our raw stories by common behavioral topics / interview questions 
	1. List of common topics:
		1. Leadership & Ownership
		2. Challenging project
		3. Mistakes & Failures
		4. Conflict resolution
		5. Constructive Feedback
		6. Dealing with ambiguity
		7. Motivation
		8. Cross functional collaboration
		9.  Persuasion
		10. Resilience / overcoming difficulties
	2. The idea is that when we hear an interview question , we can quickly understand what story from past experience to use.
3. Refine top 6-8 stories that show as much signal as possible
	1. Write some notes and thoughts about them using the STAR method ( see below , it is a framework useful for providing clear and structured answers )
	2. Prepare also an intro pitch ( e.g. Tell me about yourself ): a short intro that covers our current role, college, past work experience , optionally hobbies , what are we looking for and why this role excites us
4. Practise delivery of our stories and intro pitch 


Tips:
- Use always our stories ( not from internet )
- Don't read stories from the screen
- No blame or no complaints about past companies/teammates
- Be specific in our stories
- Balance highlighting our good qualities and teamwork in our stories ( we want to show both )
	- *we* is a dangerous word, we can use it to give context but then use I.


*STAR method* 
- Situation → what was the team
- Task → what was the team doing
- Action → how we worked together , which was the structure and which role i played
- Result → as a team what we got from this task ( es. grade / product / feedback )


*Common *
*Tell me about yourself*
Try to introduce yourself and keep it relevant ( e.g. when you started to do your job, why and what you are currently doing and why ).


*Tell me about a time you failed at work and what did you learn from it*
Don't go too into the weeds with a particular technology that they might not know: we want to clearly communicate technical topic to non technical people ( e.g. HR ).
